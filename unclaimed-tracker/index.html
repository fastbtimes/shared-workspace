Straight answers

Isn't the search free? Why pay anything?

Yes — searching and claiming your money is always free at missingmoney.com and your state sites, and you should never pay a "finder" a cut. ClaimTrail charges only for the software convenience of tracking many people across many states over time. If you only have one or two claims, use the free tier or just the free government sites directly.

Do you take a percentage of what I recover?

Never. We are not a finder/locator service. We charge a flat software subscription, period. Your recovered money is 100% yours.

Do you submit claims for me?

No. You submit each claim yourself on the official state site — that keeps your sensitive info in your hands and the claim 100% free. ClaimTrail organizes the work and generates your checklist.

Where does my data live?

In this demo, entirely in your own browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded. For a production launch you'd add an account system; see the README for how to wire a backend.
